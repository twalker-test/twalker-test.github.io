What is FabLab

Where ideas become
physical objects.

The RIT CAD FabLab is a state-of-the-art digital fabrication space supporting creative and technical work across all disciplines — design, fine arts, engineering, and beyond.


Complete a free safety orientation, book your machine online, and walk in ready to make. Materials available on-site, or bring your own.

🎓

For the RIT Community

All current RIT students, faculty, and staff may use the FabLab after completing a brief orientation.

🛡️

Trained Technicians On-Site

Staff and student workers are on hand every open hour for machine setup, file prep, and troubleshooting.

⚡

Rapid Prototyping Ready

Concept sketch to physical prototype — often in under an hour. Iterate fast and refine in the same session.

Equipment

Professional tools,
student access.

🔴

Laser Cutters

Epilog Fusion Pro & Trotec Speedy CO₂ systems for cutting and engraving wood, acrylic, leather, and fabric.

Requires Orientation
🖨️

FDM 3D Printers

Prusa MK4 and Bambu Lab X1C for rapid prototyping in PLA, PETG, TPU up to 350×350×350 mm.

Walk-in Welcome
💎

Resin 3D Printers

Formlabs Form 3 and Phrozen Sonic Mega for high-detail prints — jewelry, miniatures, complex geometries.

Requires Orientation
✂️

Vinyl Plotters

Roland and Graphtec plotters for HTV vinyl, stencil film, and specialty media up to 24" wide.

Walk-in Welcome
🪵

CNC Router

ShopBot Desktop CNC for carving and milling wood, foam, soft metals, and plastics. Bed 24×18".

Requires Orientation
🎨

Large Format Printer

Epson SC-P9000 wide-format inkjet for exhibition prints up to 44" wide on paper, canvas, and vinyl.

Staff Assisted
👕

Embroidery Machine

Brother Entrepreneur Pro X for digitized embroidery on garments and fabric panels up to 14×14".

Requires Orientation
🌈

Sublimation Printer

Sawgrass SG1000 for full-color transfer onto mugs, apparel, tiles, and polyester substrates.

Staff Assisted
💻

Design Workstations

Apple Mac Studios with Adobe CC, Rhino 8, Fusion 360, and Cura for file prep and design work.
